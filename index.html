About Me

Structured finance professional
focused on credit & execution.

Receivables-backed lending, credit documentation, and the systems that make deal execution scalable.

I’m a Business Finance Associate at BetterInvest, a fintech platform that finances film and media productions against contracted OTT and satellite receivables. I own the front end of these credit transactions, from assessment and LTV structuring to financial modelling, documentation and closure.

I graduated with a B.Com (Foreign Trade) from PSG College of Arts & Science, Coimbatore, and I’m now pursuing an MBA in International Finance at Jain University (ACCA-accredited) alongside the ACCA qualification.

Outside of work, I run Capital Story, a financial newsletter with 1,300+ subscribers, and invest actively in Indian equities.
